About

Meet Jon,
the Pan Man.

Jon spent more than 40 years as a social worker before retiring in 2024. Now he spends his days bringing cast iron back to life.

He is a proud Berkeley resident and Cal alum. Restoration is his retirement passion. Cooking is his first love—and he loves thinking about the delicious meals each pan will bring to a family’s table.
